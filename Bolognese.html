Bolognese

A rich and hearty meat sauce perfect for pasta.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced, optional
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro, optional

    • Instructions

    1. Make the dough: In a large bowl, combine the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and baking powder.
    2. Add the yogurt, warm water, and oil. Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
    3. Knead for about 5–7 minutes until the dough becomes smooth and elastic. If it's sticky, add flour 1 tablespoon at a time. If it's too dry, add water 1 tablespoon at a time.
    4. You want a soft dough that is slightly tacky but not sticking heavily to your hands.
    5. Let it rise: Put the dough in a lightly oiled bowl and cover it. Let it sit somewhere warm for about 60–90 minutes, until roughly doubled in size.
    6. Divide: Punch the dough down and divide it into 6 equal pieces.
    7. Roll each piece into a ball and let them rest for another 10 minutes.
    8. Shape the naan: Roll each ball into an oval about ⅛-inch thick. Don't worry about making them perfectly shaped. The irregular edges are part of the appeal.
    9. For garlic naan, press a little minced garlic and cilantro into one side of the dough.
    10. Cook in a very hot pan: Heat a cast-iron skillet or heavy stainless-steel pan over medium-high to high heat. You want the pan very hot before the first naan goes in.
    11. Place the naan garlic-side down if you've added garlic.
    12. Cook for about 60–90 seconds, until bubbles form and the bottom develops dark brown or charred spots.
    13. Flip and cook another 30–60 seconds. You want some nice blistering and char without drying the naan out.
    14. Finish: Brush immediately with a little olive oil and sprinkle with cilantro and a tiny pinch of salt.
