Greek Salad

Crisp vegetables, olives, herbs, and a bright Greek-inspired dressing.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef (lean)
  • 1 small onion (finely diced)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger (fresh or paste)
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes (fresh or canned)
  • 1/2 to 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or ghee

Spices

  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander (optional but recommended)
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp chili powder (adjust heat)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, then saute the onion for 4 to 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the spices and toast for 30 seconds. This is key for flavor.
  4. Add the ground beef, break it apart, and cook it through.
  5. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes.
  6. Add the peas and cook for another 3 to 5 minutes.
  7. Finish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lemon.
