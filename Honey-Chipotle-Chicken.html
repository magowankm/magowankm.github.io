Honey Chipotle Chicken

Sweet, smoky, and spicy oven-baked chicken breast coated in a sticky honey chipotle glaze.

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into roughly 1-inch cubes
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil or olive oil

Spices

  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¾ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Honey Chipotle Sauce

  • ¼ cup honey
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, finely minced
  • 1½ tbsp adobo sauce from the can
  • 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp water

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper or foil for easier cleanup.
  2. Pat the cubed chicken breast dry. Add it to a bowl and toss with the oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cumin, onion powder, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.
  3. Spread the chicken across the sheet pan in a single layer with a little space between the pieces so the chicken roasts instead of steaming.
  4. Bake at 425°F for 10 minutes.
  5. While the chicken cooks, whisk together the honey, minced chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, lime juice, soy sauce, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, apple cider vinegar, and water.
  6. After 10 minutes, remove the chicken from the oven. Flip or stir the pieces, then pour about ¾ of the honey chipotle sauce over the chicken and toss until evenly coated.
  7. Return the chicken to the oven and bake for another 5–7 minutes, or until the chicken reaches 165°F internally.
  8. Remove the pan and toss the chicken with the remaining sauce. Return it to the oven for another 1–2 minutes to heat and thicken the fresh coating.
  9. For extra caramelization, switch the oven to broil for 1–2 minutes at the end. Watch closely because the honey can burn quickly.
  10. Remove from the oven and let the chicken rest for 2–3 minutes so the sauce can thicken and become stickier before serving.
