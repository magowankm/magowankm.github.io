Sweet and Spicy Gobi

Tender roasted cauliflower coated in a savory tomato-spice mixture with a hint of sweetness and bright lemon.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole head cauliflower (cut into medium florets)
  • 2 tbsp avocado oil or olive oil
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 3–4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger (fresh, grated)
  • 1–2 tomatoes (finely chopped) or 1/2 cup canned tomatoes
  • Fresh cilantro (for finishing)
  • Fresh lemon juice (for finishing)

Spices

  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp paprika (optional, for color)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the cauliflower florets with 1 tbsp oil, a pinch of salt, and a little turmeric. Roast for 20–25 minutes, until browned around the edges.
  3. Heat the remaining 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  4. Add the onion and cook for 5–7 minutes, until softened and lightly golden.
  5. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1–2 minutes.
  6. Add the turmeric, coriander powder, chili powder, and sugar. Stir for about 30 seconds.
  7. Add the tomatoes and salt. Cook for 5–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes break down and the mixture becomes thick.
  8. Add the roasted cauliflower and toss thoroughly to coat. Cover and cook over low heat for 5–7 minutes.
  9. Remove from heat and finish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lemon juice. Taste and adjust the salt, chili, or lemon as needed.
  10. Serve warm as a side dish or alongside rice, naan, or your favorite Indian-inspired main course.
