Coconut Chickpea Curry

A creamy and flavorful curry made with coconut milk and chickpeas.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated (or ginger paste)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) diced or crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp avocado or olive oil
  • ½–¾ cup water or chicken broth
  • 1–2 tsp brown sugar, optional
  • 1–2 tsp lime or lemon juice
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro

Whole Spices

  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 small cinnamon stick

Ground Spices

  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1½ tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garam masala, plus another 1 tsp to finish
  • ¾ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder or paprika
  • ¼ tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat. Add the ½ tsp cumin seeds and cinnamon stick. Cook for 30–45 seconds, stirring frequently, until fragrant.
  2. Add the diced onion and a small pinch of salt. Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until very soft and beginning to turn golden.
  3. Add the garlic and ginger. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring frequently.
  4. Add the ¼ tsp ground cardamom, 1½ tsp coriander, 1 tsp ground cumin, ¾ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp Kashmiri chili powder, black pepper and 1 tbsp tomato paste. Cook for 30–60 seconds, stirring constantly.
  5. Add 1 cup diced or crushed tomatoes. Cook over medium heat for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes have completely softened and the mixture becomes thick.
  6. Add the full can of coconut milk, ½ cup water or broth, 1 tsp brown sugar and about ¾ tsp salt. Stir thoroughly and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 3–5 minutes.
  7. Find and remove the cinnamon stick before blending.
  8. Reduce the heat to low. Using an immersion blender, carefully blend the curry sauce until completely smooth and creamy.
  9. Stir in all of the chickpeas. Bring the curry back to a gentle simmer.
  10. Cook uncovered over medium-low heat for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally. If the curry becomes too thick, add water or broth a few tablespoons at a time until it reaches your desired consistency.
  11. Turn the heat to low and stir in 1–2 tsp lime or lemon juice. Taste and adjust the salt, acidity and heat as needed.
