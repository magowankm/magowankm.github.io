Mexican Black Beans

Smoky, creamy black beans simmered with Mexican-inspired spices, garlic, onion, and lime.

Ingredients

  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • ½ tbsp avocado oil or olive oil
  • ¼ medium yellow or white onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼–½ jalapeño, finely diced, optional
  • ⅓ cup chicken broth or water
  • 1½–2 tsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro, optional

Spices

  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • ¼ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp dried oregano
  • ⅛ tsp ground coriander
  • ⅛ tsp onion powder
  • Pinch of cayenne, optional
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a small saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and jalapeño and cook for 3–4 minutes, until softened.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
  4. Add the cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, oregano, coriander, onion powder, cayenne, salt, and black pepper. Stir and toast the spices for about 30 seconds.
  5. Add the black beans and ⅓ cup chicken broth or water. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Mash about ¼ of the beans with the back of a spoon or potato masher, then stir them back into the pot.
  8. Simmer for another 3–5 minutes, until the liquid has thickened and the beans are creamy. Add a splash of broth or water if they become too thick.
  9. Remove from the heat and stir in 1½ teaspoons lime juice. Taste and add the remaining lime juice if you want more brightness.
  10. Stir in the cilantro, if using, and adjust the salt to taste.
