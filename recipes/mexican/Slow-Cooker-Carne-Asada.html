Slow Cooker Carne Asada-Style Beef

Citrusy, garlicky slow-cooked beef with cumin and chipotle for tacos or bowls.

Ingredients

  • 2–2½ lb beef chuck roast, trimmed
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Juice of 1 orange
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • ½ cup low-sodium beef broth
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro

Instructions

  1. Season the beef with cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Optional: Sear the beef in a hot skillet with avocado oil for 2–3 minutes per side.
  3. Place onion in the slow cooker, add beef, garlic, lime juice, orange juice and broth.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW for 7–9 hours or HIGH for 4–5 hours, until the beef shreds easily.
  5. Shred or slice the beef and toss with some of the cooking liquid. Stir in cilantro.
  6. For crisp edges, spread beef on a baking sheet and broil for 3–5 minutes. Serve in tacos, rice bowls or nachos.

Note: Traditional carne asada is grilled steak. This is a slow-cooker, carne-asada-inspired variation using chuck roast for tenderness.
