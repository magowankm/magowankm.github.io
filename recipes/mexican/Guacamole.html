Guacamole

Fresh, chunky guacamole with ripe avocados, lime, cilantro and jalapeño.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Halve and pit the avocados. Scoop the flesh into a medium bowl.
  2. Mash with a fork to your preferred texture, leaving some chunks.
  3. Add lime juice, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, salt and optional cumin. Fold gently to combine.
  4. Stir in the tomato, if using. Taste and adjust lime and salt.
  5. Serve immediately with tortilla chips, tacos or bowls. To store, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface and refrigerate.
