Black Bean Turkey Nachos with Jalapeño Yogurt

Loaded tortilla chips with spiced turkey, black beans, sweet potato, melted cheese, fresh mango and a tangy jalapeño-lime yogurt.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 sweet onion, diced
  • 1 small sweet potato, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lb ground turkey or chicken
  • 1 tsp chipotle chile powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • Two 4.5 oz cans chopped green chiles
  • One 14 oz can black beans, rinsed and drained
  • 1 cup shredded pepper jack
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • 1 bag tortilla chips
  • 1 mango, cubed
  • Fresh jalapeño slices, cilantro, avocado and lime wedges to serve

Jalapeño Yogurt

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 jalapeño, deseeded and chopped
  • Juice from 1 lime
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Optional Dairy-Free Swap

Use dairy-free shredded cheese and unsweetened thick plant-based yogurt instead of the dairy ingredients.

Instructions

  1. Set the oven to 375°F and prepare a large rimmed baking sheet.
  2. Warm olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sauté the onion and diced sweet potato for about 5 minutes, stirring until their edges begin to brown.
  3. Stir in garlic briefly, then add the ground turkey. Break it into crumbles and cook until the meat reaches 165°F.
  4. Season with chipotle powder, cumin, salt and pepper. Pour in ½ cup water and let it reduce until the filling is no longer watery.
  5. Turn off the heat. Fold in the cilantro, green chiles and drained black beans.
  6. Spread tortilla chips on the baking sheet. Layer with half the shredded cheese, the warm turkey mixture and the remaining cheese.
  7. Bake for roughly 10–15 minutes, just until the cheese is melted and the nachos are hot.
  8. While the nachos bake, mix Greek yogurt with chopped jalapeño, lime juice and salt. Adjust to taste.
  9. Finish the tray with diced mango, extra cilantro, jalapeño slices, avocado if desired and spoonfuls of jalapeño yogurt. Serve immediately with lime wedges.

Yield: About 6 servings · Time: Approximately 40 minutes.
