Chicken Tortilla Soup

A hearty, smoky chicken tortilla soup with black beans, corn, fire-roasted tomatoes and lime.

Soup Ingredients

  • 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, rinsed
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (4 oz) diced green chiles
  • 1 cup corn
  • 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lime

Seasonings

  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, or to taste
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne, optional

Toppings

  • Crispy tortilla strips
  • Diced avocado
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Jalapeño slices, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Sauté onion and bell pepper for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Add garlic, tomato paste and all seasonings; stir for 60–90 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Stir in fire-roasted tomatoes, green chiles, black beans, corn and chicken broth.
  4. Nestle the chicken into the pot. Cover and simmer gently for 20–30 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F.
  5. Transfer chicken to a plate, shred with forks, then return to the pot.
  6. Add lime juice and simmer uncovered for another 5 minutes. Adjust seasoning.
  7. Ladle into bowls and top with tortilla strips, avocado, cilantro and lime.

Note: Slow-cooker option: Combine ingredients except lime juice and toppings. Cook LOW 4–6 hours or HIGH 2–3 hours, until chicken reaches 165°F. Shred and finish with lime.
