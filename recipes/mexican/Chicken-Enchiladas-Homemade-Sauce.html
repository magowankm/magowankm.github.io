Chicken Enchiladas with Homemade Red Sauce

Soft tortillas wrap around shredded chicken and black beans, then bake in a homemade red enchilada sauce. Chili powder and cumin give the sauce depth, and a splash of cider vinegar keeps it bright.

Filling

Homemade Red Enchilada Sauce

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. For the sauce, warm oil in a saucepan over medium heat and whisk in flour for 1 minute.
  2. Whisk in chili powder, cumin, garlic powder and oregano. Stir in tomato paste, then slowly whisk in broth until smooth.
  3. Simmer sauce 6–8 minutes until slightly thickened. Add salt and vinegar; taste and adjust.
  4. Toss shredded chicken with black beans, onion, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, oregano and ½ cup of the enchilada sauce.
  5. Spread a thin layer of sauce in a 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Divide filling among 8 tortillas, roll and arrange seam-side down.
  6. Pour remaining sauce over enchiladas and sprinkle with optional cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes; uncover and bake another 10 minutes until hot and bubbling.
  8. Serve with cilantro, lime, avocado or a crunchy cabbage slaw.

Tip: To freeze, assemble in a freezer-safe dish, cool completely, wrap tightly and freeze before baking.
