Homemade Baked Ziti

Hearty baked ziti layered with seasoned beef marinara, ricotta, mozzarella and Parmesan. Serves 6.

Pasta and Meat Sauce

  • 12 oz dry ziti or penne
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 24 oz marinara sauce
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp homemade Italian seasoning (recipe below)
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Cheese Layers

  • ¾ cup ricotta cheese
  • 1½ cups shredded mozzarella
  • ⅓ cup grated Parmesan

Homemade Italian Seasoning (Full Casserole Batch)

  • 1½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1½ tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ¼ tsp dried rosemary, crushed
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly oil a 9×13-inch baking dish.
  2. Mix oregano, basil, parsley, thyme, rosemary and garlic powder to make the homemade Italian seasoning. This batch is enough for seasoning the entire casserole; use about 2 tsp in the meat sauce and reserve the remainder to sprinkle into the pasta and cheese layers.
  3. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook ziti about 2 minutes less than package directions, then drain.
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Sauté onion 4–5 minutes, add garlic for 30 seconds, then brown ground beef until cooked through (160°F). Drain excess fat if needed.
  5. Stir marinara into the beef along with 2 tsp seasoning and optional red pepper flakes. Simmer 5–10 minutes. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.
  6. Toss pasta with some meat sauce. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the baking dish; add half the pasta, dollops of ricotta, some mozzarella, and a little reserved seasoning.
  7. Repeat with remaining pasta and sauce. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  8. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Remove foil and bake 5–10 minutes longer, until bubbling and golden on top.
  9. Rest 5–10 minutes before serving.

Yield: 6 hearty servings. Make ahead: Assemble, cover and refrigerate up to 24 hours; bake until heated through.
