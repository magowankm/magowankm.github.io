Pasta Fagioli

Comforting Italian-style pasta and bean soup with vegetables, tomatoes and herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lean ground beef or ground turkey (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, rinsed
  • 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, rinsed
  • 4 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1 cup small pasta, such as ditalini
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes, optional
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley or basil

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. If using meat, brown it until cooked, then drain excess fat.
  2. Add onion, carrot and celery; sauté 5–7 minutes until softened. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes, broth, Italian seasoning, oregano, beans and optional red pepper flakes.
  4. Bring to a gentle simmer for 15–20 minutes so flavors develop.
  5. Add pasta and cook according to package instructions until tender, stirring to prevent sticking.
  6. Add water or broth to loosen the soup as needed. Season with salt and pepper, then garnish with fresh herbs.

Tip: For leftovers, cook pasta separately and add to each bowl so it does not absorb all the broth.
