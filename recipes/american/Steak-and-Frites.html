Steak and Frites

Pan-seared steak with crisp oven-baked fries and a simple garlic-herb finish.

Steak

  • 2 strip steaks or ribeyes, about 8–12 oz each
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 1 tbsp dairy-free butter or olive oil
  • Fresh thyme or rosemary

Frites

  • 1½ lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes
  • 1½ tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ¾ tsp kosher salt
  • Fresh parsley, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 450°F. Cut potatoes into thin fries and soak in cold water for 15–20 minutes if time allows.
  2. Drain and dry fries thoroughly. Toss with oil, garlic powder, paprika and salt. Spread on a baking sheet without crowding.
  3. Roast 25–35 minutes, flipping halfway, until deeply golden and crisp.
  4. Pat steaks dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high heat until hot.
  5. Add avocado oil and sear steaks 3–5 minutes per side, depending on thickness and desired doneness. Add garlic, herbs and dairy-free butter near the end; spoon over steaks.
  6. Check doneness with a thermometer; USDA recommends 145°F with a 3-minute rest for whole beef steaks. Rest 5 minutes, slice and serve with fries.
