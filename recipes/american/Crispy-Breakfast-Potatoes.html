Crispy Breakfast Potatoes

A quick boil with baking soda roughens the potato cubes so they roast up golden and crisp, with fluffy centers. Go smoky with paprika and flaky salt, or use the Greek oregano blend and squeeze fresh lemon over the potatoes as they come out of the oven.

Potatoes

Smoked Paprika Seasoning

Greek Lemon Oregano Potatoes

Use this mix in place of the smoked paprika seasoning for the same 1½ lb of potatoes.

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Lightly oil a rimmed baking sheet.
  2. Bring about 2 quarts of water to a boil. Add kosher salt and the baking soda, then add the potatoes. Boil for 5–7 minutes, until the outsides soften and the cubes still hold their shape.
  3. Drain well and let the potatoes steam-dry for 3–5 minutes. Shake the colander gently to roughen the edges.
  4. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil and your chosen seasoning mix, reserving the lemon juice for after roasting. Spread the potatoes in a single layer on the baking sheet.
  5. Roast for 45 minutes, flipping the potatoes and rotating the pan halfway through, until golden and crisp.
  6. For the smoked paprika version, sprinkle with flaky salt. For the Greek lemon oregano version, squeeze the juice of ½ lemon over the hot potatoes. Serve hot.
