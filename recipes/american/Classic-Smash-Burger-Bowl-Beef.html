Classic Smash Burger Bowl Beef

Savory, peppery, garlicky ground beef with a deeply browned smash-burger crust, perfect for building a cheeseburger-inspired bowl.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (or ¾ tsp fine sea salt)
  • 1 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ¾ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp avocado oil, if using lean beef

Optional Bowl Toppings

Serve over lettuce with tomatoes, pickles, red onion, roasted potatoes, and your favorite burger sauce. Add cheese or a dairy-free alternative if desired.

Instructions

  1. Heat a cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the ground beef and press it into a relatively thin, even layer.
  3. Leave it undisturbed for 3–4 minutes to develop a deep brown crust.
  4. Flip sections of the beef and allow the other side to brown.
  5. Break the beef into smaller pieces.
  6. Add all dry seasonings and stir.
  7. Add Worcestershire sauce and Dijon mustard.
  8. Cook another 1–2 minutes until the meat is browned, slightly crispy, and fully cooked to 160°F.

Serving suggestion: Spoon the seasoned beef over your preferred bowl ingredients and serve warm.
