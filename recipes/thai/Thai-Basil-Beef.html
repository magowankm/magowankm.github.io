Thai Basil Beef

Ground beef browns quickly before getting tossed with garlic, shallot, fresh chilies, and a savory soy-based sauce. A generous handful of Thai basil gives it a peppery, anise-like aroma that comes through over jasmine rice.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Whisk soy sauce, coconut aminos, brown sugar, water and vinegar together.
  2. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook garlic, shallot and chilies for 30–60 seconds.
  3. Add ground beef and stir-fry, breaking it into small pieces, until browned and cooked through to 160°F.
  4. Pour in the sauce and cook 1–2 minutes until glossy and reduced.
  5. Turn off heat and fold in Thai basil until just wilted.
  6. Serve over jasmine rice with cucumber, lime and an optional fried egg.

Tip: Use coconut aminos or soy sauce rather than fish sauce or oyster sauce to keep this seafood-free.
