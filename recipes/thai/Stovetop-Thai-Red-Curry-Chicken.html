Stovetop Thai Red Curry Chicken

Serves 4 · About 35 minutes

Red curry paste and coconut milk make a creamy sauce with enough chili heat to warm each bite. Bell pepper and green beans add texture alongside the tender chicken, while lime and Thai basil freshen the sauce at the end.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Fry the curry paste. Heat the oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Add 2 tbsp curry paste, the garlic, and ginger. Stir for about 1 minute, until fragrant. If it starts to stick, stir in a spoonful of coconut milk.
  2. Build the sauce. Stir in the coconut milk, broth, soy sauce, and 1 tsp brown sugar. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Cook the chicken and vegetables. Add the chicken and simmer uncovered for 5 minutes. Add the bell pepper and green beans, then simmer for another 7–10 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F and the vegetables are tender-crisp.
  4. Finish with lime and basil. Turn off the heat and stir in 1 tbsp lime juice and the basil. Taste and add more lime for brightness, a splash of soy sauce for saltiness, or the remaining teaspoon of sugar if the sauce tastes too sharp. Serve over jasmine rice.

For a thicker curry, use ¼ cup broth. For a bolder sauce with a mild curry paste, fry the full 3 tbsp in step one.
