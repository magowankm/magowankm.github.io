Chewy Pumpkin Dark Chocolate Chunk Cookies

Makes: About 18 cookies  ·  Prep: 15 minutes  ·  Chill: 30–45 minutes  ·  Bake: 11–13 minutes

These pumpkin cookies have soft, chewy centers, warm cinnamon and pumpkin spice, and pockets of melted dark chocolate. Flaky salt is optional, and dairy-free butter and chocolate make the batch dairy-free.

Cookie Tips

  • Blot the pumpkin thoroughly so the cookies stay chewy instead of cakey.
  • Let the melted butter cool before mixing in the egg yolk.
  • Take the cookies out while the centers still look a little soft.

Instructions

  1. Dry the pumpkin: Spread the pumpkin purée between layers of paper towels and gently blot until thick and paste-like. Measure ⅓ cup of the blotted pumpkin.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients: Whisk the melted, cooled dairy-free butter with both sugars. Add the blotted pumpkin, egg yolk, and vanilla; mix until combined.
  3. Mix the dry ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk the flour, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, baking soda, and salt.
  4. Make the dough: Fold the dry ingredients into the wet mixture just until no dry streaks remain. Fold in the dark chocolate chunks.
  5. Chill: Cover and refrigerate the dough for 30–45 minutes.
  6. Prepare to bake: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper.
  7. Portion: Scoop about 2 tablespoons of dough per cookie onto the baking sheets, leaving roughly 2 inches between cookies. Gently flatten the tops.
  8. Bake: Bake for 11–13 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers are still soft.
  9. Cool: Leave the cookies on the baking sheet for 10 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack. Finish with flaky sea salt if desired.
