Greek Salad

Crisp vegetables, olives, herbs, and a bright Greek-inspired dressing.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Combine the tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, red onion, and olives in a wide serving bowl.
  2. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of the olive oil and the red wine vinegar. Add about half the oregano and a small pinch of salt. Toss gently, then taste before adding more salt, since the olives and feta are already salty.
  3. Set the feta slab on top. Drizzle with the remaining tablespoon of olive oil and sprinkle with the remaining oregano.
  4. Serve right away, breaking the feta into pieces at the table. Spoon some of the tomato juices and dressing over each serving.
